# 90-Day Website Validation Scorecard

| Measure | 90-day target | Evidence source | Review cadence |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Qualified non-KION founder conversations | 10+ | Conversation log | Weekly |
| Diagnostic inquiries | Establish baseline | Thank-you visits plus received inquiries | Weekly |
| Scorecard interest | Establish baseline | Resource-page and PDF-download traffic | Weekly |
| Pilot clients | Up to 3 | Signed pilot engagements | Monthly |
| Inquiry-to-conversation conversion | Establish baseline | Inquiries divided by completed conversations | Monthly |
| Conversation-to-pilot conversion | Establish baseline | Pilot clients divided by conversations | Monthly |
| Best-performing topics | Identify top 3 | Resource visits, referrals, and conversation mentions | Monthly |
