# Founder Independence Advisory Website — Design Specification

Date: 2026-07-24  
Status: Approved concept; pending final specification review  
Project owner: Edward Abiodun

## 1. Objective

Create a focused six-page advisory website that establishes Eddie as a credible
advisor to founder-led B2B businesses and converts relevant visitors into
qualified conversations for the Owner Independence Diagnostic.

The website is a credibility and conversion asset, not the primary audience
acquisition channel. LinkedIn creates discovery, YouTube demonstrates depth,
and the website connects both channels to a coherent commercial offer.

## 2. Strategic Thesis

### Positioning

Help growing, founder-led businesses reduce dependence on the owner by improving
management visibility, documenting critical workflows, and applying practical
automation.

### Primary message

> Build a business that runs on systems—not constant intervention from you.

### Differentiating idea

Founder independence is not achieved through delegation alone. It requires:

1. Reliable executive visibility into performance.
2. Documented workflows and decision rights.
3. Practical automation that removes recurring operational friction.

AI is an enabling mechanism. It is not the headline product or the organizing
principle of the brand.

## 3. Buyer, Problem, and Offer

### Primary buyer

Owner or CEO of a founder-led B2B service business with approximately 20–100
employees.

Initial addressable categories include:

- Engineering and technical service firms
- Commercial contractors
- Accounting and professional service firms
- Specialty service providers
- Logistics businesses outside the material-handling competitive ecosystem

The website should remain relevant across these categories without presenting
an indiscriminate list of industries.

### Urgent problem

The owner cannot step away because critical reporting, operating knowledge, and
decisions still flow through them.

Common symptoms include:

- Decisions routinely wait for the owner.
- Important operating knowledge lives in individuals rather than systems.
- Management reporting is slow, fragmented, or backward-looking.
- Teams lack clear decision rights and escalation rules.
- Repetitive administrative work consumes leadership capacity.
- Growth increases operational pressure rather than organizational leverage.

### Fixed-scope pilot offer

#### Owner Independence Diagnostic

**Promise:** Identify the operational dependencies preventing the business from
running effectively without the owner and produce a quantified 90-day plan to
remove the most important one.

**Scope:**

- One 60-minute owner interview
- Review of five critical operating workflows
- Review of existing management reports and KPIs
- Identification of key-person, information, and decision bottlenecks
- Owner Dependency Scorecard
- Prioritized automation and documentation opportunities
- One redesigned workflow or executive-reporting prototype
- 90-day implementation roadmap
- Executive readout

**Duration:** 10 business days  
**Pilot price:** $3,500 fixed  
**Initial capacity:** Three pilot clients

The offer must not promise complete owner independence in 10 days. It promises a
diagnosis, prioritized roadmap, and one demonstrated improvement.

## 4. Commercial Journey

The primary journey is:

1. A founder encounters a LinkedIn post, referral, YouTube video, or search
   result.
2. The website helps the founder recognize owner dependence as an operating
   design problem.
3. The visitor reads or watches a relevant founder resource.
4. The visitor completes or downloads the Owner Independence Scorecard.
5. A sufficiently qualified visitor reviews the Owner Independence Diagnostic.
6. The visitor submits a diagnostic inquiry.

The site must support visitors at different readiness levels:

- **Problem unaware:** recognize symptoms on the homepage.
- **Problem aware:** explore founder resources and the scorecard.
- **Solution aware:** review the diagnostic scope and process.
- **Ready to act:** submit an inquiry.

## 5. Website Architecture

The website contains six primary pages.

### 5.1 Home

**Purpose:** Establish relevance, articulate the problem, explain the approach,
and route visitors to either the diagnostic or founder resources.

**Required sections:**

1. Problem-led hero with primary call to action
2. Recognition section: signs the founder remains the operating system
3. Business consequences of continued owner dependence
4. Three operating levers:
   - Management visibility
   - Documented workflows and decision systems
   - Practical automation
5. Owner Independence Diagnostic summary
6. Three featured founder resources
7. Credibility and operating perspective
8. Final call to action

**Primary CTA:** Request the Diagnostic  
**Secondary CTA:** Take the Owner Independence Scorecard

### 5.2 Owner Independence Diagnostic

**Purpose:** Convert qualified interest into an inquiry.

**Required sections:**

1. Who the diagnostic is for
2. Symptoms that indicate fit
3. Fixed scope and deliverables
4. Ten-business-day process
5. What the client receives
6. Pilot price and capacity
7. What the diagnostic does not promise
8. Frequently asked questions
9. Inquiry call to action

### 5.3 How I Help

**Purpose:** Explain the broader capabilities behind the diagnostic without
creating a confusing menu of unrelated services.

**Capability areas:**

1. Executive visibility
   - KPI architecture
   - Executive reporting
   - Management dashboards
   - Forecast and performance visibility
2. Operating systems
   - Workflow mapping
   - Decision rights
   - Process documentation
   - Operating cadence
3. Practical automation
   - Reporting automation
   - Workflow automation
   - Knowledge capture
   - Applied AI where justified

Each area must be explained as a lever for owner independence, not as a
standalone technology service.

### 5.4 Founder Resources

**Purpose:** Build authority, help visitors self-diagnose, and move readers or
viewers toward the scorecard or diagnostic.

Resources are organized by executive problem rather than by publication date or
media format.

**Category A: Build a Business That Runs Without You**

- Owner independence
- Documented systems
- Customer concentration
- Transferability
- Predictable revenue

**Category B: See What Is Happening in Your Business**

- Executive reporting
- KPI design
- Management dashboards
- Decision cadence
- Forecast visibility

**Category C: Eliminate Operational Friction**

- Workflow improvement
- Practical automation
- AI-enabled processes
- Knowledge capture
- Reporting automation

**Launch content:**

- Three featured resources minimum
- No more than six embedded YouTube players on the index page
- Written executive summary for every embedded video
- Estimated viewing or reading time
- One practical next action per resource
- One related conversion path per resource

The page is curated manually. It must not become an unstructured chronological
archive.

### 5.5 About

**Purpose:** Establish trust without reproducing a résumé.

**Required content:**

- Why Eddie focuses on better-run, less owner-dependent businesses
- Relevant experience spanning commercial strategy, executive reporting,
  forecasting, competitive intelligence, analytics, process improvement, and
  automation
- Operating philosophy: business outcomes before technology
- Direct-advisor model and practical implementation orientation
- Clear boundary that client work does not use confidential employer
  information or operate within prohibited competitive areas

Claims must be factual, supportable, and consistent with employer policies.

### 5.6 Contact

**Purpose:** Collect sufficient information to assess fit without creating a
high-friction application.

**Required fields:**

- Name
- Work email
- Company
- Role
- Approximate employee count
- Primary operational dependency or recurring bottleneck
- Desired outcome

**Primary CTA:** Request the Diagnostic  
**Alternative path:** Ask a focused question

The form must state what happens after submission and set a realistic response
expectation.

## 6. Founder Resource Model

### Flagship lead resource

**Working title:** The Owner Independence Scorecard  
**Subtitle:** 15 questions to test whether your business can run without you

The scorecard should diagnose:

- Decision dependence
- Information and reporting dependence
- Process dependence
- Customer relationship dependence
- Leadership and accountability dependence

The scorecard may initially be a downloadable PDF with a simple email form.
Interactive scoring is a later enhancement and is not required for launch.

### Content reuse system

One substantive idea should become:

1. One YouTube video
2. One written resource page
3. Three to five LinkedIn posts
4. One email or newsletter item
5. One practical tool, question set, or checklist when appropriate

This keeps the three channels aligned and avoids operating them as independent
content programs.

## 7. Benchmark Framework

Use the following sites as functional references, not visual templates.

### Value Builder

Borrow:

- One unifying intellectual framework
- Assessment-led commercial journey
- Resources connected to advisory services

Do not copy:

- Exit-only positioning
- Multiple assessments at launch
- Advisor-network business model

### EOS Worldwide

Borrow:

- Problem-oriented resource ecosystem
- Practical tools paired with concepts
- Diagnostic between education and implementation

Do not copy:

- Large navigation structure
- Trademark-heavy methodology
- Resource volume unsuitable for launch

### Built to Sell

Borrow:

- One durable editorial thesis
- Founder stories as evidence
- Reuse of ideas across media formats

Do not copy:

- Exclusive focus on company sale
- Media prominence that obscures the service offer

### ghSMART founderEDGE

Borrow:

- Executive tone
- Challenge, approach, evidence, and impact structure
- Case-study discipline
- Research placed beside services

Do not copy:

- Enterprise-consulting complexity
- Claims requiring institutional scale or proprietary research

### Foundational Operations

Borrow:

- Immediate articulation of founder dependence
- Diagnosis before transformation
- Small entry engagement before broader work
- Clear objection handling

Do not copy:

- Distinctive phrases, service names, or visual identity
- A purely operational-consulting frame that excludes reporting and automation

## 8. Brand and Experience Direction

### Design principle

**Executive rigor with founder accessibility.**

The site should feel:

- Calm
- Direct
- Experienced
- Analytical
- Practical
- Human

The site should not feel:

- Like an AI agency
- Like an enterprise software landing page
- Like an exit brokerage
- Like a generic coaching website
- Like a personal résumé

### Visual direction

- Editorial, high-trust layout
- Strong typographic hierarchy
- Generous whitespace
- Restrained color palette
- Clear left-aligned reading flow
- Diagrams, scorecards, and system visuals where they clarify ideas
- Minimal, functional motion
- Limited use of photography
- No generic stock-photo hero
- No purple technology gradients
- No excessive rounded cards or decorative feature-icon grids

Exact typography, colors, spacing tokens, and responsive behavior will be
defined in the visual design system before implementation.

## 9. Voice and Messaging Rules

### Voice

- Direct and pragmatic
- Executive-level without sounding corporate
- Challenging without blaming the founder
- Specific about operating consequences
- Conservative with claims

### Messaging rules

- Lead with the business problem and desired outcome.
- Explain AI only after establishing the operating need.
- Use “founder” and “owner” intentionally; do not alternate without context.
- Treat sellability as one benefit of owner independence, not the sole goal.
- Avoid fear-heavy exit language.
- Avoid unsupported valuation multiples and ROI claims.
- Avoid jargon such as “digital transformation” unless concretely defined.

## 10. Evidence Strategy

The launch site must not imply an advisory track record that does not yet exist.

Acceptable initial evidence includes:

- Relevant operating experience
- Clearly described methods
- Demonstration prototypes
- Educational resources
- Anonymized examples only when truthful and appropriately disclosed

As pilot work is completed, add:

- Measured before-and-after results
- Anonymized or approved case studies
- Client testimonials with permission
- Specific examples of reduced reporting time, improved visibility, removed
  bottlenecks, or reduced manual effort

## 11. Functional Requirements

The launch website must:

- Be responsive across mobile, tablet, and desktop
- Meet WCAG 2.2 AA expectations for contrast, keyboard navigation, form labels,
  focus states, and media alternatives
- Provide text summaries or transcripts for embedded videos
- Use performant video embeds that do not unnecessarily load all YouTube assets
  on initial page load
- Include basic metadata, social sharing images, sitemap, and robots directives
- Use structured data where appropriate for person, professional service,
  articles, and videos
- Include privacy and cookie disclosures appropriate to the analytics and email
  tools actually used
- Provide clear success and error states for inquiry and scorecard forms
- Avoid collecting more personal information than necessary

## 12. Measurement

Initial success should be measured by commercial learning rather than raw
traffic.

### Primary measures

- Qualified diagnostic inquiries
- Scorecard completions or downloads
- Discovery conversations with non-KION founders
- Inquiry-to-conversation conversion
- Conversation-to-pilot conversion

### Supporting measures

- Resource-to-scorecard click-through
- LinkedIn-to-website visits
- YouTube-to-website visits
- Return visitors
- Video engagement on embedded resources

### First validation threshold

Within 60–90 days of launch:

- Conduct at least 10 qualified conversations with non-KION founders.
- Secure up to three pilot clients.
- Identify the messages and resource topics that produce the strongest
  qualified response.

Traffic, impressions, and follower counts are diagnostic metrics, not the
primary definition of success.

## 13. Launch Scope

### Included

- Six primary pages
- Owner Independence Diagnostic offer page
- Founder Resources index
- Three launch resources
- Owner Independence Scorecard PDF
- Contact or diagnostic inquiry form
- Basic analytics and conversion tracking
- Technical SEO foundations

### Deferred

- Interactive scorecard application
- Client portal
- Newsletter automation beyond a basic signup
- Large searchable content library
- Paid course or membership
- Multiple diagnostic products
- Extensive industry-specific landing pages
- Automated personalization

## 14. Key Risks and Controls

### Brand activity becomes a substitute for market validation

**Control:** Pair publishing with a target of at least 10 qualified founder
conversations in the first 60–90 days.

### Website language resembles category competitors

**Control:** Use the underlying market problem but create original phrases,
diagnostic dimensions, diagrams, and service names.

### Consulting activity creates employment conflicts

**Control:** Review outside-employment, intellectual-property, confidentiality,
and conflict-of-interest policies before accepting clients. Exclude KION
dealers, direct competitor dealer networks, and overlapping material-handling
strategy or intelligence work.

### Content attracts only exit-focused owners

**Control:** Lead with operational freedom and decision quality. Present exit
value as an optional downstream benefit.

### Unvalidated claims reduce trust

**Control:** Clearly distinguish experience, methods, hypotheses, prototypes,
and verified client outcomes.

## 15. Acceptance Criteria

The design is ready for implementation planning when:

- All six pages have an approved purpose and required content.
- The primary and secondary conversion paths are explicit.
- The diagnostic scope, duration, pilot price, and boundaries are unambiguous.
- The resource taxonomy supports both current YouTube content and planned
  reporting, automation, and AI decision-system topics.
- Benchmark influences and originality constraints are documented.
- Launch scope and deferred features are explicit.
- Evidence and employment-boundary rules are included.
- No unresolved placeholders or contradictory requirements remain.
